## Architecture rulings to preserve

Preserve these decisions unless a deliberate ADR supersedes them:

- provider-neutral normalized events are the only world-facing event contract;
- simulator behavior is deterministic and seeded;
- mapping semantics are theme/provider independent;
- themes skin semantic room/station keys and do not redefine activity meaning;
- crew identity is persistent and separate from provider/model/session identity;
- personality affects ambient/visual behavior only;
- persistence remains behind `DatabaseDriver`; Tauri integration belongs at the app boundary;
- replay uses injected scheduling and deterministic projection;
- world-engine core is DOM/Pixi/provider independent; rendering is a thin Pixi layer;
- real provider adapters remain passive-first/capability-driven and are never autonomously exercised;
- adapter fixture parsers are placeholders for later manual real-format validation, not proof of provider integration.
